Privacy Policy for Earth Savior Reborn

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Earth Savior Reborn is a free arcade game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Advertising

The app offers optional rewarded advertisements. You may choose to watch an advertisement to receive an in-game reward, such as additional lives. The app does not show forced advertisements between gameplay runs.

Advertisements are provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as approximate location derived from IP address, device or other identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostics. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and legal compliance. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We do not require accounts and do not directly collect names, email addresses, contacts, precise location, payment information, or user-generated content.

The app stores gameplay preferences and high scores locally on your device. These values are not transmitted to us.

Sharing

Information collected by the advertising SDK may be shared with Google and its service providers to provide advertisements, measure performance, prevent fraud, and maintain security. We do not sell personal information.

Children

Earth Savior Reborn is not directed at children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so we can address the concern.

Data Security

Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is protected in transit using industry-standard security measures. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or applicable requirements change. Updates will be posted at this page with a revised effective date.
